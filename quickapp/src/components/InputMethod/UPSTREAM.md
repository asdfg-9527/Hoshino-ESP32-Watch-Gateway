# InputMethod upstream notice

This component is a minimal, English-only `rect` QWERTY fork of
[NEORUAA/Vela_input_method](https://github.com/NEORUAA/Vela_input_method),
audited at commit `462bc948c78ed07eb48361d693cbca1cd6b5c9bf`.

The upstream MIT license is retained in `LICENSE`. Dictionary loaders, Chinese
and Japanese input modes, T9/pill-shaped/circle branches, and their assets are
